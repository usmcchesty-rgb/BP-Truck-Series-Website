Upload a driver photo, remove the background, then drag and zoom the cutout inside the 600×700 canvas. Pick a driver and click Save — the PNG is written directly to that driver's existing Photo URL path (e.g. public/assets/drivers/mark-arthur.png). Background removal runs entirely in your browser — the first run downloads the AI model (~40 MB), so it may take a minute. Photos are saved directly to Supabase Storage and appear on the live site immediately.
