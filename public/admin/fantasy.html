            Advanced / Recovery Tools
            
    These tools can modify finalized contests, scoring, standings, or published slates. Use them only to correct an error.
    

            
    
              
    Finalized Contest Corrections
    
              
    
                Reopen finalized contest
                
    Changes contest scoring review state. Does not delete lineups. Participant-visible leaderboard may change. Reversible with re-finalization.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Recalculate finalized scoring
                
    Recomputes lineup points from official results. Does not change submissions or salaries. Participant-visible scores may change. Reversible by recalculating again.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Apply manual score adjustment
                
    Opens race scoring tools below. Does not publish slates. Participant-visible scores may change. Not automatically reversible.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Force standings rebuild
                
    Rebuilds season standings from finalized race outputs. Does not modify salaries or slate publication. Participant-visible standings may change. Not reversible.
    
                
              
    
            
    

            
    
              
    Published Slate Corrections
    
              
    
                Add missing eligible drivers
                
    Adds eligible roster drivers missing from the published slate. Does not remove drivers or change existing lineups. Participant-visible driver pool may change. Reversible by removing drivers manually.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Full regenerate published slate
                
    Rebuilds every eligible driver salary on the published slate. Does not delete lineup submissions. Participant-visible salaries may change. Not reversible.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Remove driver from published slate
                
    Opens driver pool tools to review exclusions. Does not alter finalized scoring. Participant-visible slate may change. Not reversible without regeneration.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Change next race
                
    Opens draft slate generation for a different upcoming race. Does not finalize the previous contest. Participant-visible contest may change. Not reversible.
    
                
              
    
            
    

            
    
              
    Race Selection Corrections
    
              
    
                Change completed race
                
    Re-targets post-race scoring to a different completed official race. Does not publish the next slate. Participant-visible results may change. Not reversible.
    
                
              
    
              
    
                Re-import official results
                
    Reloads official finish order and identity matches. Does not publish slates or open entries. Not participant-visible until scoring is recalculated. Reversible by re-importing again.
    
                
              
    
            
    

            
    Unusual or destructive actions also remain available in the sections below. Routine post-race work should use the Fantasy Race Cycle above.
    

          
    
            
    Historical Slate Backfill
    
            Generate saved draft slates for completed races earlier in the season so public salary history and driver trend pages populate. Uses the current fantasy salary model and is approximate.
            
            
    
              
              
            
    
            
    
          
    

          
          
    
            
    Post-Race Automation
    
            Automatic fantasy scoring and next-race salary draft generation after official results. Salaries remain draft until admin publishes.
