What is BP Fantasy?

BP Fantasy is a salary-cap game for the Blazing Pedals Truck Series. Each race week you pick 5 drivers whose real-world results score fantasy points. Every driver has a salary — you must stay under the $50,000 cap.

Accounts are live. Study the Race Slate, build your lineup, and submit before lock. Standings and race scoring will populate after each race.

How the Salary Cap Works

You have $50,000 to spend on exactly 5 drivers. Top performers cost more; lower-ranked drivers cost less. A strong lineup uses most of the cap without going over.

  • Salary — the price of a driver this race week. Shown on the Race Slate and driver pages.
  • Salary change — how much a driver’s price moved since the last slate (up, down, or new).
  • Remaining salary — cap minus what your lineup spends. Aim to use most of the budget without going over.

Example: five drivers at $12,000 + $11,000 + $10,000 + $8,500 + $6,300 = $47,800 used — $2,200 left under cap.

How to Read Value Grades

Each driver gets a Value Grade (A+ through D) based on how much fantasy production you get for the salary you pay. Higher grades mean better “bang for your buck” at that price.

  • A+ / A — elite value; strong picks if you can fit the salary.
  • B+ / B — solid value; common building blocks for balanced lineups.
  • C+ / C — average or thin value; fine in small doses.
  • D — poor value at current salary; usually avoid unless you need the spend.

Grades are recalculated each slate. A driver can be an A one week and a B the next if salary rises faster than expected performance.

What Ownership % Means

Projected Ownership estimates what share of players might pick each driver. It is not a guarantee — it helps you see who is popular vs. overlooked.

  • Favorite (40%+) — many players will roster this driver; safe but crowded.
  • Popular (28–39%) — widely rostered fantasy play; still a strong projection.
  • Moderate (18–27%) — reasonable pick without being overwhelming.
  • Sleeper (10–17%) — lower-owned upside; good for differentiation.
  • Dark Horse (<10%) — rarely picked; high risk, high differentiation reward.

Favorite and Popular drivers are the obvious BP Fantasy picks many players roster. Contrarian fantasy lineups lean toward Sleepers and Dark Horses.

Active vs. Inactive

Drivers who have not started a race recently may be marked Inactive. They can still appear on the slate with a salary, but they carry extra risk — they may not race this week.

  • Active — recent starts in the league; normal availability.
  • Inactive — no starts in the recent window; shown dimmed on tables. Last start race is listed when known.

Inactive drivers cannot be submitted in your lineup. The builder warns you before you add them.

How to Use the Lineup Builder

The Lineup Builder lets you pick exactly 5 drivers under the cap. Log in to save and submit your lineup before lock time.

  • Best Overall — highest combined fantasy tier scores.
  • Best Value — prioritizes value grades over raw rank.
  • Balanced — mix of score and value; tries to spend most of the cap.
  • Dark Horse — favors strong drivers with lower projected ownership.
  • Stars and Sleepers — at least one expensive star plus a value-tier pick when available.

Tap a strategy card, review the suggested lineup, then tweak your own picks mentally using the Race Slate.

How to Compare Drivers

Use Compare Drivers to put two drivers side by side: salary, tier, value grade, ownership, activity status, and recent form. Helpful when deciding between two similar salaries.
